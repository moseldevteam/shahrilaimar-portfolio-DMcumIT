The problem

A new account with no audience and no ad budget, selling in a crowded niche. Generic house walkthroughs get scrolled past.

What I did

Put the price in the first frame (Rumah Bawah RM300k, Sri Pertiwi) and spoke to first-time buyers in Bahasa Malaysia. I kept it to 1:11 while my other walkthroughs ran from 3:39 to 5:36.

What happened

It earned the highest save count of my top videos. 46% of its viewers were returning, against 10% on my most viral video, so the audience was warmer. 10% of views came from search, people typing the project name. All 4 sales were at Sri Pertiwi, the project in this video.

What I would change

On every video most viewers leave within 2 seconds. Next batch opens on the price reveal with no intro, tests 45 to 60 second cuts, and logs every lead against its source video from day one.

What every video produced

Higher reach gave warmer leads. Low-reach videos gave cold ones. TikTok brought 15 leads. Counts per video were not tracked, so outcomes show lead temperature. Before TikTok, text-only posts on Threads brought 14 leads, all cooler. Moving from writing to video made the leads warmer.
